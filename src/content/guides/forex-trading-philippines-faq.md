Short answers to common questions, each linked to a full guide where the topic needs more explanation than a paragraph can give.

## Is forex trading legal in the Philippines?

Yes, for individual retail traders using an internationally regulated broker. The SEC's enforcement targets unregistered entities soliciting investments locally, not individual traders using regulated offshore brokers. Full explanation: [Is Forex Trading Legal in the Philippines?](/guides/is-forex-trading-legal-in-the-philippines/)

## How do I know if a broker is legitimate?

Check the broker's license number directly on the regulator's own official register (FCA, ASIC, CySEC, or similar) — never through a link the broker provides. Full steps: [How to Check If a Forex Broker Is Legit](/guides/how-to-verify-a-forex-broker-license/)

## What does it mean if the SEC has issued an advisory against a broker?

It's a public warning about unauthorized solicitation, not automatically a court finding of fraud — though it should always be taken seriously and investigated further. Full explanation: [SEC Advisory List Explained](/guides/sec-advisory-list-explained/)

## What's the difference between a regulated and unregulated broker?

Regulation requires things like segregating client funds and maintaining minimum capital, and gives you a regulator to complain to if something goes wrong. It doesn't protect you from normal trading losses. Full explanation: [Regulated vs Unregulated Brokers](/guides/regulated-vs-unregulated-brokers/)

## What is a prop firm, and is it the same as a broker?

A prop firm lets you trade its capital after passing a paid evaluation, rather than trading your own deposited funds with a broker. The consumer protections that apply to regulated brokers don't map directly onto prop firm evaluations. Full explanation: [What Is a Prop Firm?](/guides/what-is-a-prop-firm/)

## What are the most common forex scam patterns targeting Filipino traders?

Guaranteed or fixed "risk-free" returns, pressure to deposit more to "unlock" withdrawals, fake trading dashboards, and unregistered entities using "forex" as a wrapper for a Ponzi structure. Full explanation: [Common Forex Scams Targeting Filipino Traders](/guides/common-forex-scams-in-the-philippines/)

## Does high leverage mean higher profit potential?

It means larger position sizes relative to your deposit — which magnifies both gains and losses equally. It does not make a strategy inherently more profitable. Full explanation: [Leverage Explained](/guides/leverage-explained/)

## What is negative balance protection, and do I have it?

A broker policy that caps your losses at your account balance, so extreme volatility can't leave you owing the broker money. Not universal — check your specific broker's terms. Full explanation: [Negative Balance Protection Explained](/guides/negative-balance-protection-explained/)

## Should I test a broker with a demo account first?

Yes, but understand its limits — a demo tests the platform and your strategy, not real execution quality, withdrawal reliability, or the broker's regulatory legitimacy. Full explanation: [Demo Account vs Live Account](/guides/demo-account-vs-live-account/)
